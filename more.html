Mallo

Pesäpallo is the Finnish cousin to American baseball, and after 104 years it was still being scored with counting stats. I’m a longtime fan and wanted to know whether the sport could carry real analytics. Mallo is the first set of pitching, defensive, and advanced batting metrics built for it.

Play To Clinch

Earth’s first-ever team tennis computer game. College-format dual matches, a junior recruiting circuit, and GTT, a co-ed pro league, all run on one continuous rating scale. Underneath it is a deterministic point-by-point Python engine. This is the second sport I’ve stood up on a fork of that engine, and I directed its build without writing the code myself.

Game design, rating system, directed engine build

Viperball

Live

A gridiron sport I invented, with a rulebook, a probability engine that plays it, and a 280-team universe to play it in. A terminal-style stats portal serves the whole thing, on an analytics framework written for this sport’s mechanics. Another directed agent build: roughly 110,000 lines across about 60 decision records.

A reference implementation for live audio rooms on ATProto. You sign in with ATProto OAuth, create a room, publish its metadata, and connect audio through LiveKit. Fork the repo and run your own.

Occupant Indices

Live, recomputed daily

Public measurement infrastructure for the AI economy. Three live indices: a compute price deflator, a synthetic activity unit modeled on the IMF’s SDR, and a labor displacement gauge built on federal procurement and wage data. All three recompute daily from public sources, and the git history is the audit trail.
