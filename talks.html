    Beyond Civic Tech: Public Mechanics Starts at Home

    Cascadia Open Source Conference · Keynote

    Civic tech often promises more than it delivers, putting its attention on apps or dashboards while the systems that keep public life running go overlooked. Public mechanics is a framework for seeing the rules, workarounds, and repair practices that hold services together after the launch moment has passed. The keynote looks at why the “last mile” of delivery is always political and how resilient processes guard against misuse. It also makes the case that maintenance, the unglamorous part of the work, is what keeps systems durable enough to deserve public trust.
